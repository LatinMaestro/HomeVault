# Store Listing Draft

This is a working content package for Apple App Store and Google Play preparation. Final text must match the submitted production build and cleared public brand.

## Naming Gate

HomeVault remains the repository and product codename. Current stores already contain multiple apps using HomeVault or Home Vault, including overlapping home-inventory products.

Before reserving store records:

- perform Australian and intended-market trademark/legal clearance;
- confirm App Store Connect name availability;
- confirm Google Play listing differentiation;
- choose final bundle/application identifiers;
- decide whether the public name needs a distinctive qualifier.

Working public-name candidate: **HomeVault Home Care**

This candidate is not legally cleared and must not be treated as final.

## Positioning

Category: Productivity, with Lifestyle as the secondary category where supported.

Core promise: A calm, privacy-first place to keep household assets, maintenance, warranties, receipts, documents and reminders organised.

The listing must not claim:

- insurance claim automation;
- financial or budgeting functionality;
- OCR or AI extraction;
- notification delivery until implemented;
- offline household-data access until implemented;
- public record or attachment sharing;
- advanced household invitations or role management.

### Description

HomeVault Home Care helps homeowners and families keep the important details about their home close at hand.

ORGANISE YOUR HOME

- Group appliances, tools and equipment by room and category.
- Record brands, models, serial numbers and purchase details.
- Keep practical notes with each household asset.

STAY ON TOP OF MAINTENANCE

- Create recurring and one-off maintenance tasks.
- Review upcoming and overdue work from a clear home dashboard.
- Keep completion history and trusted service-provider details together.

KEEP WARRANTY AND RECEIPT DETAILS HANDY

- Link warranty information to the relevant asset.
- Track important start and expiry dates.
- Organise receipt and document details without budgeting features.
- Keep approved PDF, JPEG and PNG attachments in private household storage.

REMEMBER IMPORTANT DATES

- Record household reminder dates and linked context.
- Review due and overdue items in one attention queue.

PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN

- No advertising or behavioural analytics.
- No AI or OCR processing.
- No unnecessary camera, location or microphone access.
- Private attachments remain behind verified household access.
- Clear controls and straightforward household language.

HomeVault is designed for Australian homeowners who want practical records for maintenance, repairs, warranties and everyday home upkeep.

## Review Notes Draft

HomeVault is a household records and maintenance organiser. It does not provide financial advice, budgeting, insurance claim processing, OCR, AI extraction or paid integrations.

Reviewer access instructions and synthetic review credentials will be added after production authentication exists.

Private document attachments are implemented for PDF, JPEG and PNG files and must be claimed only after the production Blob lifecycle passes final acceptance. Notification delivery, camera capture, public links and advanced household sharing are not included.

## Privacy Disclosure Working Notes

Expected account-based production data includes:

- account name, verified email and generated user/session identifiers;
- optional household profile and physical address metadata entered by the user;
- optional asset, room, maintenance, warranty, reminder, provider and receipt metadata;
- optional private PDF, JPEG or PNG document attachments;
- support communications only if the user separately contacts support.

Current product commitments:

- no advertising;
- no cross-app tracking;
- no sale of household data;
- no behavioural analytics;
- encryption in transit for production services;
- account and associated-data deletion;
- data minimisation.

The engineering draft for Apple privacy labels and Google Data safety answers is maintained in `docs/STORE_PRIVACY_DISCLOSURE_MATRIX.md`. Final declarations must be checked against the signed production build, native SDK inventory and vendor contracts.
