# MVP Scope

## In Scope

- Home dashboard.
- Vault sections for rooms, assets and documents.
- Maintenance tasks and service records.
- Warranty records and expiry awareness.
- Receipt/document metadata.
- Settings for household-level preferences.
- Stubbed single-user auth during Phase 1.

## Out Of Scope

- Production authentication.
- Real file uploads or production storage.
- OCR, AI extraction or smart categorisation.
- Email forwarding.
- Push, SMS or email notifications.
- Payment or subscription billing.
- Native iOS or Android apps.
- Analytics and paid third-party integrations.
- Financial budgeting or spending tools.
