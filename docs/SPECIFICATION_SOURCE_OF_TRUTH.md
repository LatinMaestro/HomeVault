# Specification Source Of Truth

HomeVault is a privacy-first home maintenance, warranty, receipt, asset and reminder vault for Australian homeowners and families.

## Vision

Help households keep important home records organised, findable and useful when maintenance, repairs, warranties, insurance or seasonal upkeep need attention.

## Product Principles

- Privacy-first.
- Beginner-friendly.
- Mobile-first.
- Simple and practical.
- No unnecessary complexity.
- No financial or budgeting features.
- No AI or OCR in Phase 1.
- No native iOS app in Phase 1.
- No paid third-party integrations in Phase 1.
- No production file storage or authentication until approved.

## Target Users

- Australian homeowners.
- Families managing home responsibilities.
- Owners of appliances, tools and outdoor equipment.
- Property owners who need reliable warranty, repair and insurance records.

## Approved Phase 1A Outcome

A documented repository and deployable Next.js visual shell suitable for Vercel Preview review.
